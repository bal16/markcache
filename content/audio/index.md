# Audio

> Cara dengar musik gratis: web streaming, client desktop, radio dunia, sampai
> pengunduh lossless. Fokus yang legal-abu-abu minim iklan; hindari layanan
> yang minta kartu kredit.

## Indonesia

- [Irama Nusantara](https://www.iramanusantara.org/en) — arsip metadata musik rekaman Indonesia, dari era piringan hitam. `arsip` ⭐
- [Madrotter Treasure Hunt](https://madrotter-treasure-hunt.blogspot.com/) — blog arsip musik lawas & langka Indonesia. `arsip`
- [Radio Garden](https://radio.garden/) — jelajahi radio seluruh dunia via bola dunia; radio Indonesia ikut terindeks. `radio`

## Streaming (Web)

- [YouTube Music](https://music.youtube.com/) — katalog terbesar, termasuk lagu Indonesia & indie. `streaming` ⭐
- [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/) — musik buatan pengguna: DJ, indie, remix. `streaming` ⭐
- [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com/discover/free-music) — rilisan gratis dari artis independen. `streaming`
- [Audiomack](https://audiomack.com/) — streaming hip-hop, afrobeat, dan elektronik gratis. `streaming`
- [Audius](https://audius.co/) — streaming terdesentralisasi berbasis blockchain. `streaming`
- [JioSaavn](https://www.jiosaavn.com/) — streaming populer India & internasional. `streaming`

## Client Desktop

- [Spicetify](https://spicetify.app/) — kustomisasi & plugin Spotify: tema, lirik, ad-free. `tools` ⭐
- [Limusic](https://simohypers.github.io/limusic/) — client YouTube Music lintas platform, ringan. `client` ⭐
- [Nuclear](https://nuclearplayer.com/) — player desktop streaming gratis lintas sumber. `client`
- [Pear Desktop](https://github.com/pear-devs/pear-desktop) — client YouTube Music dengan ad-block. `client`
- [Monochrome](https://monochrome.st/) — streaming lossless di browser, tanpa iklan. `streaming`

## Radio & Lofi

- [Radio Garden](https://radio.garden/) — ribuan stasiun radio dunia dalam satu globe interaktif. `radio` ⭐
- [World Radio Map](https://worldradiomap.com/) — indeks radio per kota, navigasi wilayah. `radio`
- [Chillhop](https://app.chillhop.com/) — radio lofi & jazz-hop dengan seleksi kurasi. `radio`
- [Flow-Fi](https://www.flowfi.app/) — lofi radio dengan sesi tematik. `radio`
- [Lofi and Games](https://lofiandgames.com/) — lofi radio berpasangan dengan permainan sederhana. `radio`
- [Ambient Sleeping Pill](https://ambientsleepingpill.com/) — ambient noise sepanjang hari, pas untuk fokus/tidur. `radio`

## Unduh & Ripping

- [Lucida](https://lucida.to/) — unduh FLAC/AAC/MP3 dari Amazon Music, SoundCloud, dan lainnya. `unduh` ⭐
- [DoubleDouble](https://doubledouble.top/) — ripper Amazon Music & SoundCloud, mudah dipakai. `unduh`
- [cobalt](https://cobalt.tools/) — pengunduh video/audio lintas platform, clean, tanpa iklan. `unduh` ⭐
- [YTiz](https://ytiz.xyz/) — unduh dari SoundCloud & Bandcamp, format AAC. `unduh`
- [Musify](https://musify.club/) — unduh MP3 320kbps dari banyak sumber. `unduh`
- [FLAC Attack](https://flacattack.net/) — koleksi unduhan lossless FLAC. `unduh`

## Sinkronisasi & Self-Host

- [Navidrome](https://navidrome.org/) — server musik self-host (Subsonic API), ringan. `selfhost` ⭐
- [FunkWhale](https://funkwhale.audio/) — streaming musik federated untuk komunitas. `selfhost`
- [Nicotine+](https://nicotine-plus.org/) — klien Soulseek, sumber lagu langka & unreleased. `oss` ⭐

## Database & Discovery

- [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/) — database musik terbuka, standar metadata. `database` ⭐
- [Discogs](https://www.discogs.com/) — database rilis fisik & marketplace vinyl. `database`
- [Every Noise at Once](https://everynoise.com/) — peta genre musik dunia, eksplorasi rasa terperinci. `database`
