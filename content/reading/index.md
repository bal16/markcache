# Reading

> Tempat membaca gratis: komik, manga, manhwa, light novel, e-book, dan
> audiobook. Banyak situs ini ramai iklan, jadi pasang uBlock Origin sebelum
> berkunjung.

## Indonesia

- [Komiku](https://komiku.org/) — manga & manhua berbahasa Indonesia, update rutin. `komik` ⭐
- [maid.my](https://www.maid.my.id/) — manga Indonesia, koleksi klasik dan baru. `komik`
- [softkomik](https://softkomik.co/) — komik batch berbahasa Indonesia. `komik`
- [Voratoon](https://voratoon.id/) — manhwa dan manhua terjemahan Indonesia. `komik`
- [BacaLightNovel](https://bacalightnovel.co/) — light novel terjemahan Indonesia. `novel`
- [Sakuranovel](https://sakuranovel.id/) — novel Jepang & light novel bahasa Indonesia. `novel`
- [Pustaka Langka](https://langka.logosid.app/) — buku-buku klasik Indonesia terdigitalisasi. `buku` ⭐
- [Catatan Nusantara](https://www.catatannusantara.com/pustaka) — pustaka sejarah Indonesia. `buku`
- [Sastra.org](https://www.sastra.org/) — arsip karya sastra Indonesia klasik. `buku` ⭐

## Manga & Manhwa

- [MangaDex](https://mangadex.org/) — komunitas scanlation terbesar, gratis, perlu akun. `komik` ⭐
- [Weeb Central](https://weebcentral.com/) — agregator manga bersih, tanpa iklan agresif. `komik`
- [Comix](https://comix.to/) — koleksi manga & manhwa besar, pembaca nyaman. `komik`
- [MangaFire](https://mangafire.to/) — aggregator cepat, multi-sumber. `komik`
- [Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/) — manhwa official gratis dari Naver. `komik`
- [MangaUpdates](https://www.mangaupdates.com/) — database rilis & pembaruan manga paling lengkap. `database`

## E-book

> Untuk file yang diunduh: scan dengan antimalware sebelum dibuka.

- [Anna's Archive](https://annas-archive.gl/) — meta-search e-book dan jurnal terbesar. `buku` ⭐
- [Z-Library](https://z-library.sk/) — library e-book besar, banyak format. `buku`
- [Library Genesis](https://libgen.li/) — jurnal, buku teks, dan buku umum. `buku`
- [Mobilism](https://forum.mobilism.org/) — forum e-book, audiobook, dan majalah. `buku`
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/texts) — jutaan buku prascan publik domain. `buku`
